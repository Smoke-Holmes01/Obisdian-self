# 定积分

## 倒代换

1. $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{\ln x}{1+x^2} \mathrm{d}x$
2. $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{\ln x}{1+x+x^2} \mathrm{d}x$
3. $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{\arctan x}{1+x+x^2} \mathrm{d}x$
4. $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{\ln x}{a^2+x^2} \mathrm{d}x \quad (a>0)$
5. $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{\arctan x}{x(1+\ln^2 x)} \mathrm{d}x$
6. $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{\ln(1-x+x^2)}{(1+x^2)\ln x} \mathrm{d}x$

## 正反相加

1. $\iint_{x^2+y^2 \le 1} \frac{\arctan \frac{1+x^2}{1+y^2}}{1+e^{-y}} \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$
